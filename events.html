August 8-9, 2026

Bangkok, Thailand - Future Intelligence Hub x Adam's House

LearnHack asks young builders to improve education with imagination by building practical learning tools over two days. The program is free for accepted teams, supports non-coders through Lovable, and connects Bangkok's local education problems with FIH's global implementation narrative.

FIH Future Decade Challenge

Global online hackathon for future-world solutions age 15+

Participants choose WFIF-inspired international issues, define a 2036 scenario, build a POC website, and submit a one-minute English product pitch. Entries are grouped for middle and high school, college and university, innovators, and women-led teams.

FIH role
Provide the public challenge structure, topic bridge, review pathway, and visibility for emerging builders.
Outcome
Approved works can be listed publicly, reviewed by judges, and connected to the Bangkok WFIF conversation.
