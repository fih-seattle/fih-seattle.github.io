LearnHack 2026

August 8-9, 2026

Bangkok, Thailand - Future Intelligence Hub x Adam's House

LearnHack asks young builders to improve education with imagination by building practical learning tools over two days. The program is free for accepted teams, supports non-coders through Lovable, and connects Bangkok's local education problems with FIH's implementation narrative.

FIH role
Connect the Future Intelligence Hub framework with an active Bangkok education-prototype pathway.
Application note
Teams of three to five can apply for one of 60 places; applications close July 26, 2026.

World Future Intelligence Forum 2026

United Nations Conference Centre · Bangkok, Thailand

ESGIN will convene 250 selected international delegates across business, investment, policy and innovation to explore ten themes shaping the next decade and build meaningful cross-border collaboration.
