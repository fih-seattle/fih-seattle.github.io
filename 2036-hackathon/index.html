The challenge

Submit a 2036 world-imagination solution that can be seen, tested, and explained.

This online hackathon invites in-school students age 15 and above to create practical future-world proposals. Each team must define a 2036 scenario, explain the problem inside that scenario, build a POC website, and record a one-minute English product introduction video.

Who

Students age 15+

Open to individual students or student teams currently enrolled in school, college, or university.

Theme

The world in 2036

Choose a future social, city, education, health, climate, AI, work, finance, or community scenario.

Opportunity

Bangkok presentation pathway

Selected winners may be invited to present their work at the United Nations Conference Centre in Bangkok, Asia's UN regional headquarters venue.

Bangkok international stage

From global student ideas to a 3-minute demo opportunity at UNCC Bangkok.

The FIH Future Decade Challenge welcomes student ideas from around the world. Outstanding projects may be selected for a 3-minute demo opportunity during the World Futures Intelligence Forum in Bangkok, where an in-person hackathon review is also taking place.

The summit theme connects AI, ESG, human development, impact capital, and ethical leadership. The online hackathon gives students a visible way to bring future-world solutions into that international conversation.

Required deliverables

Every submission must include these parts.

  • 2036 scenario definition: where, who, what changed, and why it matters.
  • Problem statement and target users inside that 2036 scenario.
  • Solution concept with a clear implementation logic.
  • Published POC website URL for the solution.
  • One-minute English product introduction video URL.
  • Student/team information and school affiliation.

Submission form

Send the project links directly to FIH.

Judging criteria

Final selection combines judge scores and global voting momentum.

Projects are evaluated through five 20-point judge criteria, together with global public voting. Public votes contribute 20% of the final selection, recognizing visibility, community support, and audience engagement.

20 pts

Scenario Definition

The 2036 context is specific, plausible, and connected to real users or communities.

20 pts

Solution Fit

The proposal addresses the defined problem with a practical and understandable implementation path.

20 pts

POC Website

The website makes the idea visible, testable, and easy to understand.

20 pts

English Pitch

The one-minute video clearly introduces the product, users, value, and next step in English.

20 pts

Impact Potential

The project can create meaningful educational, social, environmental, or economic value.

Public Voting

Global Support Matters

Approved projects can rally support through public voting. Global votes contribute to final selection as a visibility and community-support signal.
