Market Pattern Intelligence for Student Investors

This proof-of-concept imagines a 2036 impact-capital learning platform that turns financial time-series behavior into visual pattern evidence university teams can inspect, discuss, and challenge.

Watch the pitch Research source
Scenario

2036 impact-capital education needs transparent AI.

University finance labs, community investment clubs, and first-time investors need tools that explain recurring market patterns without pretending that an AI forecast is certainty.

Problem

Charts are visible, but pattern logic is hidden.

Learners may recognize price movement after the fact, yet struggle to connect candlestick behavior, historical patterns, and model confidence in a structured way.

Solution

Convert sequences into inspectable images.

Inspired by the Springer research article, the demo frames financial time series as Gramian Angular Field images and uses a CNN-style classifier to surface possible technical patterns.
