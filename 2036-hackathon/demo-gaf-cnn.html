2036 Scenario

Impact-capital education needs trustworthy evidence.

By 2036, more young people will use AI-assisted investing tools. Universities and community finance hubs need a way to teach responsible decision-making without treating AI signals as certainty.

User Problem

Charts are visible, but reasoning is hidden.

Learners can see price charts, but they often cannot explain pattern logic, uncertainty, or why a model suggests one interpretation over another.

POC Solution

Turn market sequences into inspectable AI evidence.

The POC converts candlestick time-series windows into Gramian Angular Field images, then uses a CNN-style classifier to surface possible patterns with confidence and explanation prompts.
