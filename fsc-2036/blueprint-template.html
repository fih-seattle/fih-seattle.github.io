6. Proposed Solution

Explain the solution, user journey, and core functions. Add a sketch, diagram, or prototype image if helpful.

7. Future Roadmap

Show a credible path: Today → 3 years → 5 years → 2030 → 2033 → 2036. What must happen, and what is the biggest challenge at each stage?

8. Success Metrics

Give three to five measurable indicators, such as people served, emissions reduced, jobs created, access improved, or learning outcomes.

9. Risks & Assumptions

What could go wrong? Which assumptions are most uncertain? Would the idea still help if the future develops differently?

10. Why Now?

Why must work begin in 2026 rather than waiting until 2036?

11. Global Collaboration

Which governments, universities, NGOs, companies, investors, communities, or other partners would be needed?

Optional Inspirations

List books, studies, news, lived experience, or other sources that informed the idea. This section is not scored.
