The challenge

Submit a 2036 world-imagination solution that can be seen, tested, and explained.

This online challenge invites young innovators and changemakers age 12-30 to create practical future-world proposals. Each participant or team must define a 2036 scenario, explain the future problem, submit an FIH Future Blueprint, and record a one-minute English concept video.

Who

Participants age 12-30

Open to individual participants and teams of two to five members, with all members between ages 12 and 30.

Theme

The world in 2036

Choose one of ten future-solution themes and use it as the starting point for a concrete 2036 scenario.

Required deliverables

Every submission must include the Future Blueprint and English video.

  • FIH Future Blueprint PDF, one to two pages, organized around future context, problem, roadmap, and impact.
  • One-minute English concept video explaining the future scenario, problem, solution, and expected impact.
  • Participant information: name, age, group, school or organization, country or region, and team member list if applicable.
  • AI tools disclosure explaining which AI tools were used and how they supported the work.
  • Public display choice: public showcase and voting, or private judge review only.
  • Optional bonus materials such as slides, sketches, diagrams, websites, prototypes, or additional videos.

Judging criteria

Final selection combines review scoring and public support.

Final results combine reviewer scoring (80%) and public support signals (20%). Review evaluates future thinking, problem definition, solution design, impact potential, feasibility, and communication.

20 pts

Future Thinking

The 2036 scenario is specific, plausible, evidence-informed, and more than a description of today's situation.

15 pts

Problem Definition

The pain point is real and clearly explained, including why current solutions are insufficient.

20 pts

Solution Design

The proposed solution responds to the 2036 scenario with clear logic and persuasive design choices.

20 pts

Potential Impact

Success metrics are specific and measurable, with meaningful social, environmental, or economic value.

10 pts

Feasibility

The roadmap from today to 2036 is concrete, time-bound, and realistic.

15 pts

Communication

The one-minute English video clearly explains the future scenario, problem, solution, and expected impact.

Public Voting

Community Support Matters

Approved projects can rally support through public voting. Public votes contribute 20% of the final result as a visibility and community-support signal.
