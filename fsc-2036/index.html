The challenge

Submit a 2036 world-imagination solution that can be seen, tested, and explained.

This online challenge invites young innovators and changemakers age 12-30 to create practical future-world proposals. Each participant or team must define a 2036 scenario, explain the future problem, submit an FIH Future Blueprint, and record a one-minute English concept video.

Participants age 12-30

Open to individual participants and teams of two to five members, with all members between ages 12 and 30.

Why is there an entry fee?

The fee supports the work required to operate one entry through the competition: eligibility and completeness checks, submission administration, the qualified-reviewer and results process, public-voting administration for entries that opt in, and preparation of official digital recognition for qualifying award recipients.

What does the payer receive?

  • Processing of one eligible individual or team entry.
  • Access to the published competition framework and Future Blueprint structure.
  • Qualified reviewer assessment under the published criteria, with conflict-of-interest safeguards, after eligibility review.
  • Public showcase and voting eligibility if public display is selected.

Judging criteria

Final selection combines review scoring and public support.

Reviewer scores determine Gold, Silver, Bronze, and Honorable Mention recognition for eligible public entries. A separate combined result—80% reviewer performance and 20% verified public support—is used for overall public ranking, People's Choice, and selected showcase opportunities.

20 pts

Future Thinking

The 2036 scenario is specific, plausible, evidence-informed, and more than a description of today's situation.

15 pts

Problem Definition

The pain point is real and clearly explained, including why current solutions are insufficient.

20 pts

Solution Design

The proposed solution responds to the 2036 scenario with clear logic and persuasive design choices.

Success metrics are specific and measurable, with meaningful social, environmental, or economic value.

10 pts

Feasibility

The roadmap from today to 2036 is concrete, time-bound, and realistic.

15 pts

Communication

The one-minute English video clearly explains the future scenario, problem, solution, and expected impact.

Public Voting

Reviewer qualifications and conflict safeguards

Reviewers will be selected based on relevant professional, academic, industry, education, policy, innovation, or community experience. Individual reviewer identities may remain confidential to protect reviewer independence, reduce outside influence, and support candid assessment. Reviewers must disclose relevant conflicts of interest and may not evaluate an entry where impartiality could reasonably be questioned.

Results provided to participants

Participants will receive the final reviewer score or recognition result applicable to their entry after results are finalized. Individual written comments, detailed feedback reports, score explanations, or reviewer correspondence are not guaranteed unless separately announced in writing.

Public-voting integrity

Public support contributes 20% of the combined result for public entries and does not replace qualified reviewer assessment. Voting activity may be audited, adjusted, or excluded when abnormal, duplicated, automated, purchased, coordinated, or otherwise manipulated activity is detected. The organizer may request verification before confirming results.

How scores are used

Reviewer scores determine Gold, Silver, Bronze, and Honorable Mention recognition. For public entries, a separate combined result—80% reviewer performance and 20% verified public support—is used for overall ranking, People's Choice, and selected showcase opportunities. A public entry with a reviewer score of 90 or above remains eligible for Gold recognition even if its public-support result is lower. The organizer will publish the public-support normalization method before voting opens.

Information collected and purpose

FIH collects identity, age eligibility, contact, school or organization, location, team, project, AI-disclosure, publication-choice, referral, and status information to administer eligibility review, communication, judging, voting, recognition, and the competition record.

Systems and limited sharing

Submission records are stored in Firebase. A registration summary is processed through FormSubmit to notify the organizer and copy the participant. Reviewers receive only the information reasonably needed to assess eligible entries. Information is not sold or provided to third parties for unrelated commercial marketing.

Retention, security, and payment data

FIH will use reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect submission records and retain them only as reasonably needed for competition operations, integrity review, recognition, disputes, and applicable recordkeeping. Card numbers, bank credentials, and other sensitive payment credentials must not be submitted through this form and will not be stored in the public competition database.

Non-refundable payment policy

Entry fees are generally non-refundable after payment. No refund is provided for change of mind, voluntary withdrawal, failure to complete or maintain required materials, missed deadlines, disqualification for rule violations, loss of public-voting eligibility, dissatisfaction with scores or results, or inability to attend an optional in-person opportunity. Payment does not guarantee an award, ranking, certificate, publication, invitation, or other optional benefit.

A correction or refund may be issued for a verified duplicate payment, an incorrect amount charged, an unauthorized transaction, cancellation by the organizer without delivery of the paid competition service, or when otherwise required by applicable law or the payment provider's binding rules. Nothing in this policy limits non-waivable consumer or billing-dispute rights.

When paid service begins

Payment authorizes FIH to begin performance immediately by activating the entry, recording payment status, scheduling or allocating review, and administering the entry within the competition. For a participant under 18, the parent or legal guardian must review and accept the payment terms and complete or expressly authorize payment.

Private and public entries

Pending entries remain private from other participants. A participant who selects public display permits publication of the approved project title, participant or team name, scenario summary, video link, and approved materials. Private-review entries receive qualified reviewer assessment only and are not eligible for Gold, Silver, Bronze, Honorable Mention, Special Awards, People's Choice, certificates, public voting, website showcase, public award listings, or Bangkok representative selection.

Bangkok showcase cohort rule

The Bangkok representative-selection process will proceed only if the organizer determines that the eligible, payment-confirmed cohort is sufficiently competitive and suitable for meaningful selection. The organizer may cancel or withhold that process if the cohort is too small or does not meet the required quality, fit, or presentation-readiness standard. No age group, topic, award recipient, or participant is guaranteed an invitation, and live submission totals are not published during the open call.

Corrections, withdrawal, and data requests

Before the submission deadline, participants may request a correction or withdrawal by emailing the organizer from the address used to register and including the submission ID. Pre-registration may be withdrawn without charge before payment. Data-access or deletion requests will be handled subject to competition administration, fraud prevention, payment, and legal recordkeeping needs.

Rule changes and material notice

FIH may make reasonable changes needed for security, fairness, law, scheduling, or event operations.
